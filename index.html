What we do

Daily Dose of GTA VI is an independent fan channel about Grand Theft Auto VI. Every day we publish short videos that explain official news, compare the game's state of Leonida with real-life Florida, and put the numbers behind the launch into context. Every claim in a video is checked against official or reputable sources, and the sources are listed in the video description.

From launch day, 19 November 2026, we also feature one community clip every day: players send in their best GTA VI moments, we pick one, and we publish it with full credit to the creator.

Get your clip featured

  1. Record your GTA VI moment (PS5: Create button, Xbox: Share button).
  2. Send it through our submission form with the name you want to be credited with.
  3. No story spoilers, no leaks and nothing that breaks Rockstar's rules. We review every clip before it is posted.
  4. If your clip is picked, it appears as Clip of the Day with your name on screen and in the caption.
