Privacy Policy

Daily Dose of GTA VI website and DDOGTAVI Uploader · Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data is collected, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept and which rights you have when you visit this website (gabriel-ags.github.io/ddogtavi, "the Website"), submit a clip, or use the DDOGTAVI Uploader ("the Uploader", together "the Services"). The Services are operated by the owner of the Daily Dose of GTA VI channels ("we", "us"), based in Germany. Contact: dailydoseofgtavi@gmail.com.

1. Who the Uploader is for

The Uploader is a private tool. It is used by the channel owner to publish the channel's own finished videos to the channel's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. There is no public sign-up. If anyone else logs in, their data is processed as described below and can be deleted at any time (see section 8).

2. Data we collect

a) When you log in with TikTok (Login Kit)

With your permission, TikTok sends us an access token and the following data for the scopes you approve:

  • user.info.basic: open ID, display name and avatar, shown on the posting page so you can see which account will post.
  • video.publish: permission to upload and publish the videos you select to your account, with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings you choose. We also receive the creator settings TikTok returns (available privacy levels, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, maximum video length) and the publish status of each post.
  • user.info.stats: follower, following, like and video counts of your own account.
  • video.list: the list of your own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

b) When you connect YouTube or Instagram

Google OAuth (YouTube Data API: uploading and managing the channel's own videos and comments) and Meta login (Instagram content publishing for the connected professional account). We receive access tokens and the data needed to upload, schedule and manage the owner's own posts and to read their basic performance numbers.

c) Clip submissions

If you submit a clip through our form (hosted by Tally), we receive the clip, the credit name you choose, an optional profile link and your email address.

d) Visiting the Website

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and time of access for security and operation; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. The posting page uses your browser's session storage only to hold a one-time login security value (OAuth "state") until the login is finished.

3. How we use the data

  • To show which account is connected and to publish the videos you select with the settings you choose.
  • To show the publish status and to read the account's own performance numbers for content planning.
  • To credit clip submitters and contact them about their clip.
  • To keep the Services secure and working.

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions with legal effect, and we never sell it.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Providing the Uploader you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); your consent given on the platform's login screen or in the submission form (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time; and our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)).

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with the platform it belongs to, to perform the action you requested (for example, a video is sent to TikTok when you click Post), and with the providers that host the Services (GitHub for the Website, Tally for the submission form, Google for email). We do not share, sell or rent personal data to anyone else, unless required by law.

6. Storage, security and retention

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, never on the Website or a public server, and are sent to the platforms only over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • The app's client secret never leaves the owner's computer.
  • Tokens are kept until access is revoked or the account is disconnected, and are then deleted. TikTok access tokens expire after 24 hours, refresh tokens after 365 days.
  • Account and video statistics are kept for up to 24 months for content planning, then deleted.
  • Clip submissions are kept while the clip is published and are deleted on request.

7. International transfers

The platforms and providers above may process data outside the European Union, for example in the United States. These transfers rely on the providers' own safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

8. Your rights and how to revoke access

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time. Email dailydoseofgtavi@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions → remove "DDOGTAVI Uploader".
  • Google: Google account permissions.
  • Instagram/Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites.

After you revoke access, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

9. Platform policies

TikTok features are also subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy. YouTube features use YouTube API Services and are subject to the Google Privacy Policy; by using them you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Instagram features are subject to the Instagram Privacy Policy.

10. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the European Union), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the current version. Material changes are announced on this page before they take effect.
